Gemma decides what. Code decides whether it's allowed.

How MERA turns your own reset history into one small, safe thing to do away from the screen.

On your phone

Your history → facts

Past resets and notes stay in IndexedDB. MERA turns them into a few small signal ids:

cat_helped:movement:2:2
act_helped:walking:2
note_theme:fresh_air

Settings filter first: low mobility means no walking facts are sent at all.

Open Gemma

Gemma writes one mission

LOCAL AI · Gemma 3 1BIn the browser (WebGPU / WASM). Nothing leaves the device.
CONNECTED AI · Gemma 4 26BOpt-in, on Workers AI. Gets the feeling, settings and signal ids. Never notes text.

Returns JSON: the mission, a one-line why, and which facts it used.

→
Plain code

Every answer is checked

✓Schema (Zod), short, plain words
✓Safety: no traffic, strangers, night walks, strenuous exercise, screens
✓Settings: indoor-only, low mobility, no sound
✓The why may only cite facts MERA sent, with numbers that match
Fails? One repair try, then the built-in library of 23 checked missions. Raw model text is never shown.
